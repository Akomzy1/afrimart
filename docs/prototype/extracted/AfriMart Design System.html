i.
Foundation

A heritage grocer, brought home

AfriMart carries the foods of home to the African diaspora across the United States. The system dresses that promise in the language of a fine provisions house — editorial, confident, unhurried. Every screen should feel considered, never crowded; premium, never cold.

01

Editorial calm

Generous whitespace and a clear typographic hierarchy. The page breathes before it sells.

02

Quiet luxury

Deep green and ivory lead. Terracotta and gold appear only where they earn attention.

03

Architectural, not bubbly

Crisp corners, hairline borders, still-life imagery. Structure over decoration.

04

Dignified voice

Warm and plain-spoken. We reassure in full sentences and never raise our voice.

ii.
Colour

Deep green, warm ivory, a hairline of gold

Market-green anchors the brand. Terracotta is the single accent for the most important action on a screen. A restrained metallic gold is reserved for moments of ceremony — seals, dividers, the Taste of Home boxes.

iii.
Typography

A high-contrast serif, led by a humanist sans

Cormorant Garamond carries display headlines and product names — elegant, editorial, timeless. Hanken Grotesk handles every label, control and paragraph. Small-caps, letterspaced, mark sections and eyebrows.

v.
Surface & motion

Crisp corners, soft shadow, gentle motion

Radii stay subtle — architectural, never pill-shaped. Shadow appears only where something genuinely lifts. Motion is calm and quick, with no bounce.

Corner radii — crisp, not rounded

Borders are a single warm hairline (1–1.5px, #E7DCC9). Focus adds a green edge with a soft terracotta ring. Gold hairlines mark ceremony only.

Motion — 120 / 200 / 320ms on cubic-bezier(.2,.6,.2,1). Buttons nudge 1px on press; bars animate their fill; the recipe agent shows a typing indicator. No bounce, ever.
vi.
Two surfaces

One system, two apps

The buyer shops; the merchant fulfils. Both are mobile-first PWAs on a 430px frame, drawing from this one system — fixed header, scrolling body, fixed tab bar.

Buyer PWA

The shop, unhurried

A familiar grocery app — search, browse, cart, reorder — with an optional recipe-to-cart agent. Trust signals throughout: verified sellers, the freshness promise, honest multi-parcel shipping.

vii.

Warm, plain-spoken, dignified
